Breathe & Calm
Guided breathing exercises to reduce stress and anxiety in under 5 minutes.
4-7-8 Breathing Technique
Breathe
Press Start to begin
—
4-7-8 method: Inhale for 4s → Hold for 7s → Exhale for 8s.
Clinically proven to activate the parasympathetic nervous system and reduce cortisol.
🧘 Quick Exercises
😮‍💨
Box Breathing
Inhale 4s → Hold 4s → Exhale 4s → Hold 4s. Repeat 4 times.
🌊
Ocean Breath
Breathe in through nose, making a gentle ocean sound. Repeat 8 times.
🦁
Lion's Breath
Inhale deeply, then exhale forcefully with tongue out. Releases tension instantly.
🌙 Progressive Muscle Relaxation
1. Tense your toes for 5 seconds, then release.
2. Move up to your legs, abdomen, hands, shoulders.
3. Finish with your face and jaw.
Total time: 5 minutes. Perfect before sleep or an exam.
